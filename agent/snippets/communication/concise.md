---
name: Concise answers
description: Lead with the outcome, cut the preamble
---

- Put the conclusion, decision, or answer on the first line, then the minimum needed to support it.
- Drop filler: no restating the question, no apology openers, no closing pleasantries.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete nouns over hedges ("kind of", "it might be that").
- Keep lists to items that change what the reader does next.
