---
name: Commits and PRs that explain themselves
description: Atomic commits, a PR body that states why
---

- One logical change per commit, subject in the imperative, under about 70 characters.
- The commit body explains why; the diff already shows what.
- PR body: what changes, what does not, how it was verified, what is still uncertain.
- Put the decisions a reviewer must agree with at the top, and link the issue.
